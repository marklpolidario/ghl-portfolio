01 Featured build

Automated lead capture, routing & follow-up

A real GoHighLevel system I built for a Louisiana roofing & exteriors company, shown white-label. Watch the full walkthrough, including a live form test.

Real client build

One link in, every lead followed up

Homeowners, contractors, subcontractors and partners all start from one link or QR code, and the system takes it from there.

  • One link, four doors: short forms in English or Español, with hidden fields that track which event and QR code each lead came from.
  • Persona Router: sends each lead to the right person, tags language and event, creates the pipeline card and a follow-up task, and keeps test contacts away from the team.
  • Four pipelines: Sales, Contractor Network, Recruiting and Partnership, so the owner sees every job, crew and deal stage by stage.
  • Follow-up on autopilot: same-day booking emails and call tasks for high-intent homeowners, a year of nurture emails for everyone else, stopping the moment they book or reply. Text steps are built and switch on after carrier approval.
  • Mia, the AI receptionist: answers missed and after-hours calls in English or Spanish, books free inspections, transfers calls and emails the office a summary.
24s from form submit to the team's high-intent email alert, in the live test shown in the video.
